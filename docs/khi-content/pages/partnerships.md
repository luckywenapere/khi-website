# Partnerships

Kind Humanitarian Initiative prioritizes strategic partnerships to broaden its impact and address diverse community needs. These collaborations are built on a foundation of shared values and a commitment to creating sustainable positive change.

## Margaret Umeche Centre

Margaret Umeche Centre is an organization that focuses on women's education and empowerment, with programs that include menstrual hygiene education, and education on the negative aspects of social media, and collaborates with other organizations to perform these programs.

## Ramadan Food Aid

Ramadan food aid is aimed at providing FOOD-STUFFS to the poor, orphans, widows and the needy during the month of Ramadan and beyond. It started since 2021 in Abuja with plan to extend to all states of the federation.

## Kenvic Global Foundation

Kenvic Global Foundation (KGF) is a non-profit organization that empowers marginalized groups, especially women and children, through education, skills development, and leadership growth.

## Hopeful Hands for Help Initiative

Hopeful Hands for Help Initiative (Hinitiative) is an organization dedicated to inspiring hope and transforming lives through a focus on sustainable development, education, healthcare, and social justice. Their work centers around empowering communities, advocating for the rights of vulnerable populations, and creating opportunities for individuals to thrive.
