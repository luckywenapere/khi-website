# Board Of Trustees

## Board of Trustees

The Board of Trustees at Kind Humanitarian Initiative comprises visionary leaders, seasoned professionals, and passionate advocates. Guided by a shared commitment to sustainable development and ethical governance, our board provides strategic oversight, ensures financial accountability, and champions innovative solutions to drive lasting social impact. With diverse expertise in humanitarian work, education, public health, and social entrepreneurship, our trustees play a pivotal role in shaping policies, fostering partnerships, and safeguarding the integrity of our programs.

### Meet The Team

## Engr. Oluwaseun Ige Abolurin

Chairman

Engr. Oluwaseun Ige Abolurin is a registered engineer, a licensed electrical inspector, a Certified Management consultant and a certified Field Service Manager with professional experience in various sector which includes power & energy, process audit, E-Mobility and renewable energy.
 
 
Engr. Abolurin holds a bachelor’s degree in electrical engineering from the University of Ilorin, an Executive MBA from Rome Business School, Rome, Italy, an MBA from Universidad Internacional de Valencia, Valencia, Spain.
 
 
He is presently the Head Technical support Africa at Polarium Energy Solutions AB. He is a teacher of the word of God with great passions for volunteering to help the less privileged.

## Dr. Oluwaseun Yinka Alabi

Secretary

Dr. Oluwaseun Yinka Alabi is a seasoned administrator, engineer, and project manager with extensive experience in various sectors, including health, finance, and diplomacy. He holds a Doctoral degree from Atlantic International University, USA, and degrees in Electrical Engineering from the University of Ilorin and Federal University of Technology, Akure, both in Nigeria.
Dr. Alabi is a Fellow of the Nigerian Society of Engineers and holds prestigious professional qualifications, including Certified Facility Manager and Project Management Professional. He is currently the Head of Real Estate Administration and Projects at African Export Import Bank, leading high-level project initiatives.
Outside professional engagements, he is a strong advocate for leadership development, passionate about mentoring, and developing young adults. He is a Pastor in Deeper Life Bible Church and the Founding Executive Director of Kind Humanitarian Initiative, a non-governmental organization focusing on youth development, girl child education, and women empowerment.
Dr. Alabi has authored several scholarly articles and presentations on infrastructure development, facilities management, and leadership empowerment at local and international forums. He is a dedicated professional with a commitment to making a positive impact in Africa.

## Ekpa Friday Okpanachi

Member

Ekpa, Friday Okpanachi is a Barrister and Solicitor of the Supreme Court of Nigeria and a Law Teacher/Researcher at the Faculty of Law, Prince Abubakar Audu University (Kogi State University), Anyigba, Nigeria. He currently serves as the Coordinator of the Prince Abubakar Audu University Law Clinic. 
 
Ekpa holds an LL.B (Hons) degree from Kogi State University and a B.L. from the Nigerian Law School, and he was called to the Nigerian Bar over a decade ago. He obtained an LL.M and is currently pursuing his PhD in Law at the University of Ibadan, Nigeria. 
 
As an active member of the legal community, he is affiliated with several organizations, including the Nigerian Bar Association, the Nigerian Association of Law Teachers (NALT), the Environmental Law Research Institute (ELRI), the Association of Environmental Lawyers of
Nigeria, and the Network of University Legal Aid Institutions (NULAI) Nigeria. 
 
An avid researcher, Friday has published numerous works in books and peer-reviewed journals, focusing on Environmental Law/Rights, Petroleum Law, Energy Law and Climate Justice, the Nigerian Legal System, and Clinical Legal Education. He has served as Editor-in-Chief of the Lokoja Bar Journal of Law and Practice and is a member of the editorial board for several peer-
reviewed journals. 
 
In addition, he has participated in various conferences, colloquiums, workshops, and seminars, where he has presented papers. Over the years, he has been a staunch advocate for access to justice, providing free legal representation for the underprivileged through the Law Clinic.

## Omobola Omofaiye

Member

Omobola Omofaiye is a visionary business leader with extensive experience leading teams across Sub-Saharan Africa and Europe. She holds a bachelor's degree in Electrical Engineering from the University of Ilorin, Nigeria and a Global Executive MBA from Manchester Business School, UK. Her industry experience spans the Power and Energy management sector, Renewable energy sector, Telecommunication sector, Financial Service Institutions and Oil and gas sector.
 
 
Omobola has held leadership roles in several blue-chip multinationals, including Schneider Electric, Somfy and Vodacom. She was responsible for end-to-end management of business operations and technical sales at Schneider Electric and Country Head of West and Central Africa for Somfy.
 
 
She also served as Chief Commercial Officer at Arnergy, developing and steering solar business operations. Currently, she is the General Manager at Levene Renewable Energies Division, driving strategic leadership and growth.
