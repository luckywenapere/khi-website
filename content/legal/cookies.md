# Cookie Policy

**Effective Date:** 7th March, 2025

**1. Introduction**

This Cookie Policy explains how Kind Humanitarian Initiative ("we," "us," or "our") uses cookies and similar tracking technologies on our website, https://khinitiative.org (the "Site"). By using our Site, you consent to our use of cookies as described in this policy.

**2. What are Cookies?**

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work, or work more efficiently, as well as to provide information to the owners of the site.

**3. How We Use Cookies**

We use cookies for the following purposes:

* **Essential Cookies:** These cookies are necessary for the operation of our Site. They enable you to navigate the Site and use its features, such as accessing secure areas.
* **Analytics Cookies:** These cookies allow us to analyze how visitors use our Site. They help us understand which pages are most popular, how visitors move around the site, and how we can improve our Site. We use this information to improve the user experience.
* **Functionality Cookies:** These cookies allow our Site to remember choices you make (such as your language preference) and provide enhanced features.
* **Third-Party Cookies:** We may also use third-party cookies for analytics or other purposes. These cookies are subject to the respective privacy policies of the third parties.

**4. Types of Cookies We Use**

* **Session Cookies:** 
 + These are temporary cookies that exist only for the duration of a user's session on a website.
 + They are typically used to remember user actions within a single browsing session, such as keeping items in a shopping cart or maintaining login status.
 + These cookies are deleted when the user closes their browser.
* **Persistent Cookies:** 
 + These cookies remain on a user's device for a specified period, even after the browser is closed.
 + They are used to remember user preferences, such as login information, language settings, or website customizations, across multiple sessions.
 + They can also be used for tracking user behavior over time.
* **First-Party Cookies:** 
 + These cookies are set by the website that the user is currently visiting.
 + They are used to enhance the user's experience on the website, such as remembering user preferences or enabling essential website functions.
* **Third-Party Cookies:** 
 + These cookies are set by domains other than the website that the user is currently visiting.
 + They are often used for advertising and tracking purposes, allowing third parties to collect data about user browsing behavior across multiple websites.
 + This data can be used to deliver targeted advertisements.
* **Essential (Strictly Necessary) Cookies:** 
 + These cookies are crucial for the basic functionality of a website.
 + They enable users to navigate the website and use its features, such as accessing secure areas or completing online forms.
 + Without these cookies, the website may not function properly.
* **Analytics Cookies:** 
 + These cookies collect data about how users interact with a website, such as which pages they visit, how long they spend on each page, and how they navigate the website.
 + This data is used to analyze website traffic and improve the user experience.
 + Common examples include Google Analytics cookies.
* **Functionality Cookies:** 
 + These cookies enable websites to remember user choices and preferences, such as language settings or font sizes.
 + They enhance the user's experience by providing personalized features.
* **Marketing/Advertising Cookies:** 
 + These cookies track user browsing behavior to deliver targeted advertisements.
 + They may collect data about user interests and demographics to personalize ads.

**5. Managing Cookies**

You can control and manage cookies in several ways:

* **Browser Settings:** Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can typically choose to accept, reject, or delete cookies. Please refer to your browser's help documentation for instructions on how to manage cookies.
* **Third-Party Tools:** Some third-party tools allow you to manage cookies and tracking technologies.
* **Opt-Out Options:** Some third-party services provide opt-out options for their cookies.
